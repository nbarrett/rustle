# Rustle iPhone and iPad privacy policy

Updated 12 October 2026.

Rustle is an on-device speech-to-text app developed by Nick Barrett. This policy covers the standalone iPhone and iPad beta.

## Speech and transcripts

Rustle accesses the microphone only for a recording you start. Speech is processed locally with Whisper. Audio is held in memory for transcription, is not saved as a recording by Rustle, and is not uploaded to the developer or a transcription service.

Transcripts, history, preferences and correction rules are kept in the app's local storage. You can edit or clear transcript history in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to Apple's device backup and restore behaviour.

When you tap Copy transcript, Rustle writes the selected transcript to the device clipboard so you can paste it into another app. The privacy policy of that destination applies to text you choose to paste or send there. iCloud and Universal Clipboard behaviour is controlled by your Apple settings.

## Model downloads

The initial speech model download requires an internet connection and retrieves model data from Hugging Face. The hosting provider receives ordinary download connection information, such as your IP address. Rustle does not include your recordings or transcripts in that request. Once a model is downloaded, speech transcription can run without an internet connection.

## Accounts, analytics and advertising

Rustle does not require an account and includes no advertising, developer analytics or tracking. Apple may collect TestFlight usage and crash information under its own TestFlight privacy terms. If you submit feedback or a screenshot through TestFlight, Apple makes that feedback available to the developer. Review screenshots before submitting them if they contain private text.

## Support

For privacy questions or app support, contact the developer through https://github.com/nbarrett/rustle/issues. Do not include private recordings or sensitive transcripts in a public issue.
